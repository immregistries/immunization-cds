# Version 0.1.0 Drafting Notes — October 29, 2026

Version 0.1.0 was created as a deliberately imperfect starting point. It
reorganizes the narrative content of the September FHIR IG into a conceptual
guide that can be reviewed independently of the FHIR publication format.

## What was carried forward

- Stateless forecast architecture and system boundary.
- Request and response concepts from the proposed operation.
- Contextual Conditions as optional asserted inputs.
- Knowledge Base selection and disclosure.
- Evaluation, recommendation, transparency, error-handling, and capability
  discovery subject areas.
- The FHIR resource mapping, examples, and open technical questions.

## What changed

- The canonical document is broader than a FHIR implementation guide.
- Detailed FSH artifacts were not copied; the existing FHIR IG remains the
  technical source for them.
- Contextual Conditions are linked to their dedicated project rather than
  redefined in full.
- “Schedule Authority” is visibly provisional.
- The conflict over use of `Observation.effective[x]` was marked rather than
  silently resolved.
- Claims of settled cardinality or representation were softened into working
  proposals and open questions.

## Known gaps

- The intended October 29 InteropHub meeting notes were not present when this
  version was drafted.
- Original ImmDS compatibility has not been compared requirement by
  requirement.
- The February narrative and historical decks have not been fully adjudicated.
- No publication-quality diagram or complete FHIR payload example is included.
- The initial PowerPoint source uses the newly released shared v0.3.0 template
  support. Its two diagram slides are intentionally text-first and need visual
  review before presentation.
