# Version 0.2.0 Rebaseline Notes (10 November 2026)

Version 0.2.0 reorganizes the Topic Reference around the scope language on the
HL7 Public Health Immunization Decision Support page, as a working preview for
the 12 November 2026 topic 1 meeting.

## What changed from 0.1.0

- Thirteen chapters merged into five plus the decisions appendix.
- Scope rewritten from the "Latest Proposed Language": the two use cases,
  including testing and validation, which 0.1.0 did not cover; continuity with
  ImmDS 1.0.0; international basis; complementary efforts.
- Contextual Conditions content cut down to the parts topic 7 leaves to this
  topic, with links to topic 7 for definitions.
- Material from the September and October 2026 meeting notes, and the open
  FHIR change requests, added.
- Terms table and four Mermaid diagrams added as first drafts.
- WD-8 to WD-19 added. OQ-2, OQ-4, OQ-10, and OQ-11 resolved. OQ-13 to
  OQ-19 added.
- Schedule Authority and Knowledge Base are now defined here, not in topic 7
  (WD-11). Topic 7 links to these definitions and keeps the terminology
  steward. OQ-1, on renaming Schedule Authority, is open here.
- SITT FITS, formerly NIST FITS, documented as the target testing platform
  (WD-19).

## Not yet done

- The infographic text is a first draft.
- Presentation brief under `products/presentation/` still reflects 0.1.0.
- Topic 1 overview in InteropHub not yet updated to the scope language.
- Meetings without notes (July, August, and 15 October 2026) contributed
  nothing.
